### Sales test

Show the same missed-slot incident to three operations leads. Offer one fixed-scope setup call if they can name a repeated cost or failure. Validate interest in an editorial setup session; recognize revenue when the session is delivered and paid.

### Measurement and decision

Use `dispatch-console-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The private console remains private. This empty demo contains no account connection, post queue or operating logs. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
